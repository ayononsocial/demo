Questions

Is this the official Dhaka College app?

No. DC Legacy is an independent student project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or run by Dhaka College.

What happens to my college portal password?

You type it into the college portal's own login form on your phone, so it goes only to the portal, never to DC Legacy. If you choose to remember it, it is stored encrypted on your phone only.

Why does portal sync need the Android app?

A website can't sign in to another website for you. The Android app can open the college portal on your phone and read your own pages.

Who can sign up?

Students, club members and alumni, with any email address. Pick your role when you sign up.
